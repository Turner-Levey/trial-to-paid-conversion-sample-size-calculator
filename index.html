What It Estimates

The A/B mode estimates trials needed to detect a target conversion-rate lift with a two-sided normal approximation. The estimate mode sizes one cohort for a selected confidence interval width.

Why Trial Lag Matters

A trial can enter the test today and convert later. Calendar readout adds the entered trial-to-paid lag after enough qualified trials are enrolled.

Before Reading A Test

Keep the cohort definition fixed, avoid mixing traffic sources with different intent, and decide the target lift before looking at results.
